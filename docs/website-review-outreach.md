# Free Marked-Up Website Review

Use this name on the first mention in each channel and each standalone email.
Buttons may use **Get My Free Review**.

The deliverable is a screenshot with a few practical opportunities marked
directly on the page. Do not describe it as an audit, consultation, strategy
session, or video review. Lead generation is an intended outcome, not a
guaranteed result.

## Delivery process

1. Accept the website address and email without requiring a phone number,
   booking, or sales call. The website's optional phone form is separate from
   the completed review request.
2. Review the homepage and mark a few specific, practical opportunities on a
   screenshot. Explain what could improve clarity, trust, or ease of contact.
   Keep the suggestions useful on their own; do not include a proposal,
   pricing package, or promised performance gain.
3. Email the completed screenshot before inviting the recipient to a meeting.
   Attach the image or provide a direct link they can open without booking a
   call. Check that the image is legible and the attachment or link works.
4. Keep any invitation to talk optional and make it only after delivery.
   An optional phone number does not make a call part of receiving the review.

Replace bracketed placeholders and check all claims before sending. These are
manual templates; this repository does not configure an automated review
delivery or outreach sequence.
