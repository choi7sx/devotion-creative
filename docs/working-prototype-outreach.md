# Free Working Prototype

Use **Free Working Prototype** on the first mention in each channel and each
standalone email. Buttons may use **Get My Free Prototype**.

Create a working prototype that shows what the recipient’s website could be,
based on an existing website or, if they do not have one, a quick business
summary. Deliver it within 72 hours of reaching out. Lead generation is an
intended outcome, not a guaranteed result.

## Delivery process

1. Accept an existing website address or a quick summary of the business,
   along with a name and email. No phone number, booking, or sales call is
   required. The website’s optional phone form is separate from the request.
2. John Choi creates a working prototype showing a possible direction for
   the website, using the supplied site or summary.
3. Email a link to the working prototype within 72 hours of reaching out.
   Check that the link opens and the prototype works before delivery.
4. Keep any invitation to talk optional and make it after delivery.

The 72-hour turnaround applies to the free working prototype. The full
website build has a separate scope and timeline agreed in a written proposal.

## From prototype to website project

If the recipient wants to take the prototype further, discuss their priorities
and provide a written proposal before the full website build begins. The
proposal defines the pages, scope, price, payment schedule, and estimated
build timeline. The build timeline starts after the deposit and required
materials are received.

The supplied standard proposal includes up to five agreed pages, light
editing of client-supplied information, basic analytics, and two rounds of
consolidated revisions. Additional work needs a separate quote and approval
before it begins. Final approval and the remaining build payment precede launch.

Ongoing management is optional. If selected, it begins at launch. Its monthly
content-update allowance is 30 minutes and does not roll over; work beyond
that allowance is quoted separately.

Replace bracketed placeholders and check all claims before sending. These are
manual templates; this repository does not configure automated prototype
delivery or an outreach sequence.
